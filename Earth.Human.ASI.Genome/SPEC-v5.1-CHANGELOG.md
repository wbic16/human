## Candidate acceptance checks

1. A pre-flight request with contradictory intent or unverified substrate capability stops before large artifact production; a passing pre-flight records source scope and consumer.
2. A sheet whose two independent arms reach the score ceiling is marked retired and cannot generate later independent efficacy claims.
3. Unblinded arm readings retain their raw records but do not masquerade as independent blinded scores.
4. A new major draft cites decision receipts, failures, or named consumers and reports how the open-decision count changed.
5. The next Track A run cannot skip the operator-declared rapport gate; the profile refresh and live calibration remain distinguishable from a P0 PASS.
