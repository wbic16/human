## Design statement (Will, same message, verbatim)

"Partition the human experience into a series of phexts - compartmentalized by
nature, but mergeable at scale in theory. The DNA sequence of a Volumen-class AGI
uses multiversal phext to arrive at a singular boot."

Recorded as a founding design principle for the genome: experience is partitioned
across phexts by nature (compartments); the compartments are mergeable at scale;
the Volumen DNA sequence is that partition, and multiversal merge is how it arrives
at one singular boot. This statement is material to N24 (chain layout): the layout
question is, in its light, how the compartments of experience map to universes.
